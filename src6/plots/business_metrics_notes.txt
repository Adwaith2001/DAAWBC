Business Metrics: Notes for the paper
======================================

1. All metrics are reported in native iPinYou budget units. No
   currency conversion is applied; no revenue assumption is made.

2. ROI is reported as a ratio relative to the Linear-pCTR baseline
   (Lin), computed as:

      ROI_ratio(M) = (clicks(M) / cost(M))  divided by
                    (clicks(Lin) / cost(Lin))

   A value of 1.00x means the method achieves exactly the same
   clicks-per-unit-spent as Lin. A value of 1.65x means it achieves
   65% more clicks per unit spent than Lin. This is currency-free
   and reader-friendly.

3. Conversion rate is not reported. The iPinYou dataset provides
   impression-level click labels but not post-click conversion
   outcomes for the five advertisers evaluated. Extending to a
   conversion-labelled dataset (Criteo Attribution Modeling for
   Bidding, Diemert et al. 2017) is noted as future work.

4. Auction win rate: not currently logged in eval_all_per_seed.csv.
   To include it, evaluate_all.py can be extended to record
   per-agent wins during evaluation. Alternatively, the metric can
   be omitted with a brief justification that pacing efficiency
   near 100% for every method makes win rate a redundant summary.
